# Practice Exams

Three sets of original, exam-style questions for **DEA-C01 (exam guide v1.1)**. Answers are hidden in collapsible blocks. Each explanation covers why the right answer is right, why every distractor is wrong, which signal words decide the question, the skill ID it tests, and a link to the guide that teaches it.

## How to take them

1. **Simulate the real thing:** 130 minutes for a full exam, no notes, no searching. Flag questions and come back to them.
2. **Write your answers down first**, and only then open the answer blocks. Peeking as you go inflates your score and hides your real weak spots.
3. **Score by domain** using the table at the end of each exam. The real exam is compensatory, but a weak domain costs points on every form of the exam.
4. **Review every question you got right by luck** as seriously as the ones you missed.
5. **Go back to the guide**, not just the explanation. Each answer links to the guide section whose question patterns train that decision.

## Reading your score

| Score (first attempt) | What it means |
|---|---|
| **≥ 80%** (52+/65) | Likely ready. Book the exam and keep reviewing traps |
| **70–80%** | Close. Re-study your two weakest domains, then retake after a few days |
| **< 70%** | Go back through the guides for the domains you missed most, and do the labs in the [study plan](../STUDY-PLAN.md) |

The real exam reports a scaled score (pass = 720/1,000), which isn't a straight percentage. Treat practice scores as a readiness signal, not a prediction.

> These questions are written from the public exam guide and AWS documentation. They are **not** taken from the real exam, and they reflect AWS services as of September 2026.
